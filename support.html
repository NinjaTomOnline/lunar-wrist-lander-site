Connect your Apple Watch

Open Lunar Wrist Lander on your iPhone and the Wrist Lander app on your paired Apple Watch. Keep the iPhone game open during flight. When the watch is out of reach, you can fly from the iPhone using its throttle and rotation sliders.

Fly from the watch

Turn the Digital Crown to adjust thrust and tilt your wrist to steer. Use Burst for a quick boost and Stabilize to level the craft. Flick your wrist or double-tap the cockpit to abort. The Brief button on the iPhone reopens the mission instructions.

Get help

Email support@ninjatomapps.com. Include your device models, iOS and watchOS versions, and what happened before the issue appeared.
