Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Lunar Wrist Lander iPhone app and its Apple Watch companion, provided by NinjaTom Apps.

Motion and gameplay

The watch processes motion readings to steer the lander and detect the abort gesture. Apple Watch and its paired iPhone exchange flight commands and game state using Apple’s Watch Connectivity framework. Raw motion samples stay on the watch. The iPhone stores whether you have seen the mission brief in the app’s local settings.

Data collection

The apps have no accounts, advertising, tracking, or analytics. NinjaTom Apps does not receive your motion readings, flight commands, or gameplay state.

Support and privacy questions

If you email support, NinjaTom Apps receives your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond. For privacy questions, email support@ninjatomapps.com.
